01About

I'm Paulo Cauca, a cybersecurity senior specialist based in Curitiba, Brazil. I've spent close to two decades in technology, and the path was not a straight line: I started in telecom systems and data engineering, spent years running BI and big-data platforms at national scale, then moved into infrastructure and security leadership — and stayed there because that's where the problems I find most interesting live.

Today I work in security engineering for cross-border payments at EBANX, after building and leading the information security function there — certifications, governance, identity, and the culture work that makes any of it stick. Before that I built a cybersecurity team from zero at Ligga Telecom, and led BI, big data and traffic platform teams at Vivo and GVT.

I'm also the owner of three companies — Concept Clin, ClinApp and Ryzam Secure — which is where most of my thinking about healthcare software and security-as-a-product gets tested against real customers rather than slides.

Two things have stayed constant across all of it: I like teams that get better while they ship, and I believe business is a legitimate place to do good, not just a way to fund it later — more on that further down.
